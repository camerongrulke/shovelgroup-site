Shovel Group

Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026. Shovel Group is a service of Reliable Sno Solutions LLC.

This Privacy Policy explains how Reliable Sno Solutions LLC, doing business as Shovel Group ("Shovel Group," "we," "us") collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you use our website, apps and services (the "Services"). It applies to Customers, Providers and visitors.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number, password (stored in hashed form) and profile photo.
  • Property information: addresses you add, nicknames, driveway size, access instructions, gate codes and, if you book for someone else, that person's name and phone number.
  • Payment information: payment card details are collected and stored by our payment processor, Stripe. We receive only limited information such as card brand, last four digits and expiration date.
  • Provider information: home base address, service area, equipment, availability, application answers, agreement acceptance and payout details (bank information is collected by Stripe, not stored by us).
  • Communications: messages sent through in-app chat, support conversations, ratings and reviews.

Information collected automatically

  • Order and usage data: orders, times, prices, cancellations, ratings, pages viewed and features used.
  • Location data: for Providers, the device location while they are available for or completing jobs, used to send nearby offers and show customers their Provider's progress. Customers may choose to share location to fill in an address.
  • Photos: before and after photos taken by Providers at the job site.
  • Device and log data: IP address, browser type, device identifiers, operating system and cookies or similar technologies used to keep you signed in and secure the Services.

Information from others

  • Sign-in providers: if you sign in with Google, Amazon or LinkedIn, we receive your name and email address from that service.
  • Referrals: if a friend refers you, we record the referral so both of you can receive any credit.
  • Weather and mapping services: we use public weather data and mapping or geocoding services to estimate snow depth and locate properties.

2. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • Provide the Services, including matching orders with nearby Providers, processing payments, sending status updates and enabling chat.
  • Estimate snow depth, arrival times and prices.
  • Send transactional messages by email, text and in-app notification, and, where permitted, storm alerts and promotional messages.
  • Provide customer support and resolve disputes, including reviewing photos and messages.
  • Keep the Services safe, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce our Terms.
  • Pay Providers and administer promotions, credits and referrals.
  • Analyze and improve the Services and develop new features.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as described here:

  • Between Customers and Providers: Providers see the Customer's first name, the property address, instructions, photos of the property and the Customer's rating. Customers see the Provider's name, photo, rating and live location while the Provider is on the way. Phone numbers are not shared directly.
  • Service providers: companies that help us operate, such as payment processing (Stripe), email and text delivery, hosting, mapping, weather and analytics, under contracts that limit their use of the information.
  • Legal and safety: when we believe disclosure is required by law, or necessary to protect the rights, property or safety of Shovel Group, our users or the public.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing or sale of assets, subject to this Policy.
  • With your consent: for any other purpose you agree to.

3A. Text messages and your mobile number

We use your mobile number to send the text messages you agreed to receive (see our Terms of Service, section 6A) and to help Providers and Customers coordinate jobs without sharing phone numbers directly. We do not sell, rent or share mobile numbers or text message opt-in data and consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text message originator opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third parties, except the text message delivery provider that sends messages on our behalf. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, or HELP for help.

4. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and to secure your account, and limited analytics to understand how the Services are used. You can control cookies in your browser settings, but disabling essential cookies may prevent you from signing in.

5. Data retention

We keep personal information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Services. We keep order, payment and tax records for as long as required by law (generally seven years). Photos and chat messages are kept for up to two years after the related job unless needed for a dispute. When information is no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it.

6. Security

We use administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect personal information, including encryption in transit, hashed passwords, access controls and audit logging of administrative actions. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access and update: you can review and update most account information in the app.
  • Notifications: you can turn off email, text and storm alerts in account settings. Reply STOP to any text to opt out of texts.
  • Download your data: choose Download my data in Account settings to get a copy of your information.
  • Delete your account: choose Delete account in Account settings, or contact support@shovelgroup.com. We delete or de-identify your information right away, except where we must keep it for legal, tax, fraud-prevention or dispute purposes.
  • Blocking and reporting: you can block other users and report people, messages or reviews from inside the app.
  • Colorado residents: under the Colorado Privacy Act, you may have the right to confirm whether we process your personal data, access it, correct it, delete it, obtain a portable copy, and opt out of targeted advertising, sale of personal data and certain profiling. We do not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising. To exercise your rights, email support@shovelgroup.com. If we deny your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision, and if you are not satisfied with the appeal you may contact the Colorado Attorney General.
  • Residents of other states: you may have similar rights under your state's law. Contact us to exercise them.

8. Children

The Services are not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you by email or in the app before they take effect. The version and date at the top of this page show when it was last updated.

10. Contact us

Questions or requests about privacy? Email support@shovelgroup.com.
